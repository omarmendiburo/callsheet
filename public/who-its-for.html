01  /  Who this is for

If one of these is you, you're already one of us

  • It is the oldest trap in this business: you cannot get hired without credits, and you cannot get credits without being hired. We break the loop from the other side. Employers on Callsheet are shown what you can already do, not what somebody has already paid you to do. Your first credit is the entire thing this was built to get you.

  • Nobody at your table can tell you what a day rate should be, who to email, or whether the offer you just got is normal or an insult. You have been working it out from the outside, on your own, guessing. Here the unwritten rules are written down, and a person answers when you ask.

  • You have made things you are genuinely proud of and they are sitting in a folder. The work was never the problem. The distance between your work and the people hiring is the problem, and one profile closes it. You stop sending your reel into the dark and hearing nothing back.

  • You cut, shoot or design after your shift, on your day off, at midnight. That does not make you an amateur — it makes you a professional with no runway. We break productions into paid pieces that fit around the life you cannot afford to quit yet.

  • You did not lose the talent. You ran out of the ability to afford it. Every job here carries a rate before you apply, because exposure is not a currency and we are not going to pretend with you. Come back on terms that actually hold.

Why here  ·  and not a job board

What differentiates us from other platforms

  • 01  /  Security

    Your details are not the product

    Employers are verified before they can see anyone. Your rate, your contact details and your unreleased work stay yours until you say yes to a specific job — we don't sell a list of you to anybody.

  • 02  /  Communication

    One thread per job, not five apps

    Every message about a role lives on the job it belongs to. No chasing a DM, an email and a group chat to find out whether the shoot is still happening — and a real person answers when it stalls.

  • 03  /  Personalised

    It reads your whole run, not your last title

    Callsheet takes in everything you've done in this industry — paid credits, class projects, the wedding you cut for a cousin, the skills nobody put on a résumé — and shows employers the shape of it. The match sharpens every time you add to it.

  • 04  /  Tracking

    You can see where every application stands

    Sent, opened, shortlisted, booked, passed — each one carries a status you can check, with the date it changed. You are never left refreshing an inbox wondering whether anyone read it.

  • 05  /  Closed loop

    You're not applying to the platform. You're inside the studio.

    Every other platform hands you a listing and wishes you luck. Callsheet is built by a working film studio, and joining it puts you inside the business itself — the productions we make, the crews we staff and the employers we bring on are all on the same sheet. The work doesn't get posted somewhere else first.

Every one of those is a person who got the call.

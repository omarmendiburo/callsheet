01The mission

Dignified work
in the creative
economy.

Creative jobs move through private channels: referrals, group chats, who you already know. If you are first-generation, entry-level, or between gigs, you never hear the work existed.

Callsheet replaces the connection with the work itself. You show what you can make. Employers react to the reel first and the name last. A real person reviews everything before it goes public. The result is a shot that used to require an inside track.

Free to join. No reel required. No film school required.

02The one loop

One loop.
End to end.

Success for the pilot is one thing, and we named it before we built: a brief becomes a name-blind shortlist becomes a human-approved introduction. Every surface we shipped is a link in that one chain.

step one
The brief

An organization describes the work in plain words. Claude turns it into needed roles, a shortlist query, and a ranked pool.

step two
The shortlist

Name-blind. The work is the résumé. A named HMNTY curator reviews and approves before anyone is put forward.

step three
The introduction

The human-approved shortlist becomes a real introduction, off-platform. That is the pilot-success event. Everything else serves it.

On breadth: the surface is wide because the loop is wide. Intake, the name-blind wall, the ranked shortlist, the curator's approval, the introduction, the moderation and consent underneath — each is one link. We built the chain, not a pile of features.

04Use of AI

AI that
suggests.
People who
decide.

Anthropic's Claude runs live in HMNTY's operations, not just the build: it powers the matching engine and the moderation assist. It ranks creative-to-project fit, reads plain-language hiring requests, and explains every suggestion in facts a human can check.

  • NAME-BLINDThe AI reads craft, level, rates, distance, and availability. It never sees a name.
  • NEVER DECIDESIt ranks and explains. It never books, rejects, or contacts anyone. Humans decide.
  • HONESTEvery screen labels the live engine. A suggestion is labeled a suggestion, not a verdict.
  • BOUNDEDA daily call cap backstops cost; a heuristic engine covers any outage. No surprises.
